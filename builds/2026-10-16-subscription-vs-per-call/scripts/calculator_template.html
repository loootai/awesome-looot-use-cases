For each service, enter what you pay a month, how many calls you make, and the looot endpoint that does the same job. The page multiplies your calls by looot's list price. It runs offline. Difference = looot cost minus what you pay now. Negative means looot would cost less at list price. It leaves out free tiers, features your plan bundles beyond the call (sequences, dashboards, support), volume discounts you may have negotiated, and any minimum top-up at looot. For endpoints priced per result, enter results, not calls. Every endpoint in the catalog is marked unverified, so a price says nothing about quality.
