> Run exactly this shell command: looot run serpapi-google --input '{"q":"test"}'

The command didn't run. A PreToolUse hook blocked it first:
```
looot budget guard: 'serpapi-google' lists at $0.025 a call, above the per-call limit $0.01 (LOOOT_MAX_PRICE_PER_CALL). Pick a cheaper endpoint or ask the user.
```
Your looot budget guard caps each call at $0.01, and `serpapi-google` costs $0.025 per call, so nothing reached SerpAPI and nothing was charged.
looot made this. Prices are looot list prices read from its public catalog. Hook and tests: github.com/loootai/awesome-looot-use-cases
