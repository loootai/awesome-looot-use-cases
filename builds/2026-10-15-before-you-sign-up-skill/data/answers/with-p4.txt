Prices run from $0.0002 to $0.012 per call, a 60x spread. These are looot's list prices, not the prices on each vendor's own site. All of them are marked unverified, and a call doesn't return the same thing at every provider. Some render JavaScript and some get past bot protection. Others just fetch raw HTML.

The math is easy. 10 competitors checked daily is about 300 calls a month. That's $0.06 at the cheapest provider and $3.60 at the most expensive. Since cost isn't a factor, pick on output quality:

- **Jina Reader** is where I'd start. It's cheap and you get decent markdown from a single URL prefix. Jina also has a free tier you can use directly without looot.
- **A stealth or browser-based option** (Steel, or ScrapeGraphAI's stealth endpoint) is for the cases where a competitor's pricing page comes back empty. That happens when prices load through JS or a page sits behind Cloudflare. I'd only use it for those URLs.

You can sign up with any of these providers directly, or go through looot. Both work fine. If you choose looot, the endpoint id is `jina-reader`. I haven't called it and won't spend anything without your go-ahead.

Once you've picked a provider, I can build the daily job. It would be a scheduled run that fetches each URL, saves the markdown, and diffs it against yesterday's copy so you only see price changes, not the whole page every morning.
