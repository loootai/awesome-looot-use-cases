Providers differ on input (name and domain vs. LinkedIn URL), on whether they verify the address, and on hit rate for senior people at big companies. For one lookup at Stripe, I'd go with a provider that verifies the address, like Hunter, Findymail or LeadMagic, over the cheapest one.

These are looot's list prices, not the prices on each vendor's own site, and looot marks every row "unverified". You have two ways in:

- **Sign up with one provider directly.** Hunter, Tomba and Findymail all have free tiers that cover one lookup. You get an API key, then make one call with first name, last name and `stripe.com`.
- **Go through looot.** One account covers all the providers above. Tell me which endpoint id you want, and I'll stop there until you approve any spending.
