> I want Google search results for 50 keywords from a Python script. Which API should I sign up for?

Without the skill (first lines of the answer)

I'd sign up for Serper.dev. At your volume it's probably free.

Why not Google's own API. Google closed the Custom Search JSON API to new customers in 2025 and plans to shut it down for everyone on January 1, 2027. Even before that it was a weak fit. It searches a "Programmable Search Engine" you configure, so results don't always match what google.com shows, and the free tier is capped at 100 queries a day.

Third-party SERP APIs. These scrape real Google results and return JSON:

| API | Free tier | Paid | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Serper.dev | ~2,500 queries on signup | about $1 per 1k queries | Cheapest and simplest. Covers 50 keywords many times over. |
| SerpApi | ~100 searches/month | from ~$75/month | Most polished. Parses lots of SERP features (ads, maps, shopping, People Also Ask) and has an official Python library. |
| DataForSEO | small trial credit | pay as you go, fractions of a cent per query | Best if this becomes bulk SEO work: thousands of keywords, rank tracking, search volume. |

I'm quoting those prices from memory, so check each pricing page before you commit.

What to pick:
- For a one-off or occasional run of 50 keywords, use Serper.

Prices run from $0.0009 to $0.025 per call, a 28x spread. At 50 keywords, one results page each, that's about $0.05 at the cheap end and $1.25 with SerpApi. At that volume price barely matters, so pick on output instead. A call doesn't return the same thing at every provider. Some give only the top 10 organic links. Others also parse ads, People Also Ask and local packs, or hand back raw HTML. Check that the fields you need are there.
